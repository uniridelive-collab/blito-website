Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
Open the app, go to the login screen, and tap "Forgot Password". Follow the instructions sent to your registered email or phone number.

How do I update my profile picture?
Log in to your account, go to the Profile section, and tap on your current picture to upload a new one.
